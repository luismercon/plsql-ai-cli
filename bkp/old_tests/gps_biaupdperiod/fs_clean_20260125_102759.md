Procedure: gPS_BiAUpdPeriod

## General Description: 
This procedure is used to update or initialize a BiA period for a specific customer. It takes into account the order destination and delivery policy to calculate the appropriate book and inventory quantities.

## Business Rules (SBVR format):

1. Only updates the period if it exists, otherwise initializes it
2. Enforces business rules for updating BiA period based on order destination and delivery policy

## Logical Flow (procedural narrative):

- Check if the BiA period exists for the given customer and period
- If it doesn't exist, initialize it with default values from the gps_customer_bia table
- Calculate the book and inventory quantities based on the order destination and delivery policy
- Update the BiA period with the calculated quantities
