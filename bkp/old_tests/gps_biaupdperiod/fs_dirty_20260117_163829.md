Procedure: gPS_BiAUpdPeriod

## General Description: 
This procedure is used to update the Balance In Advance (BIAs) for a specific customer and period. It takes into account various factors such as order destination, delivery policy, and inventory.

## Business Rules (SBVR format):

1. Check if the delivery policy matches '[FWL]', 'T' or 'C'
2. Verify if the order destination is 'INDENT' or 'PRJIND'
3. Ensure the customer exists in the gps_customer_bia table
4. Confirm that the period (ano/mes) exists in the gps_bia_period table
## Logical Flow (procedural narrative):

- Initialize variables
- Determine the type of update based on the function argument
- Update the corresponding BIAs based on the order destination and delivery policy
- Check if the period exists in the gps_bia_period table, initialize it if not
- Update the BIAs for the specified customer and period in the gps_bia_period table
