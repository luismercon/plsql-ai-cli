Procedure: gPS_BiAUpdPeriod

## General Description: 
This procedure updates the BiA Period for a specific customer. It checks if the order destination is 'INDENT' or 'PRJIND', and updates the indent book and inventory accordingly. It also checks the delivery policy and updates the direct, transit, and call-off bookings and inventories as necessary. If the period does not exist, it initializes a new one.

## Business Rules (SBVR format):

1. Checks if the order destination is 'INDENT' or 'PRJIND'
2. Updates indent book and inventory based on order destination
3. Checks delivery policy and updates direct, transit, and call-off bookings and inventories
4. Initializes a new period if it does not exist
